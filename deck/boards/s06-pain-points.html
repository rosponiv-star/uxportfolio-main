Sixteen pain points, six people.

Still the home as it is today: what goes wrong, and for whom. With little time for the project, only the highest priorities were taken on: the six highlighted, ranked by how many personas share them and by the analysis of the domain.

WHY SIX PERSONAS

Everyone has a home, so anyone is a good persona: we wrote as many as it took to cover the needs of something everybody lives in. Four stand for the primary users, two for the secondary ones, because life at home is tied to the people around it: leaving them out would have narrowed the view of the pain points.
