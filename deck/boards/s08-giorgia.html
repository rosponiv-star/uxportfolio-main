Giorgia

The absent-minded artist

PRIMARY USER

Lives in her creative chaos: forgets lights, keys and deliveries, and feels unsafe alone.

HER STORY

Giorgia lives in a small flat that is also her studio. With brushes, boxes of paint, tools, canvases and cables everywhere, the place is always a little messy, but to her it is “creative order”.

She is brilliant but distracted: she often gets lost in her thoughts or absorbed in her work, and does not notice what happens around her.

When someone rings the intercom, she does not hear it or forgets to go and open. She orders materials online and does not notice the courier has come, then finds the notice in the letterbox.

Rushing out to a delivery or a meeting, she wonders if she left a lamp or a heater on in the studio, only to come home and find that what she had forgotten were the door keys.

Though she spends a lot of time at home, Giorgia never feels quite at ease: she lives alone, and a sudden noise, or someone ringing in the evening, can scare her.
