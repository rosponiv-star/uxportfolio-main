      00About How I work

      How I approach design

      Three habits I come back to in every project, from the first question to the last pixel.

      1. 01

        Start from evidence

        Who the people are, what gets in their way, and what the data says about it. I don’t design until I can state the problem in one sentence.

      2. 02

        Structure before style

        Content, flows and hierarchy come first. A product that makes sense is already halfway to being a product people enjoy.

      3. 03

        Craft the details, then dare

        Spacing, states, motion and microcopy decide whether people trust a product. Then I push a little further, and test whether it holds up.
