# 05 · AuraWake — facts

Sources: `Media/05-AuraWake/AuraWake1.pdf` (15 slides, dark presentation) and `AuraWake2.pdf` (18 slides, the
fuller report). Italian. "Progetto di Rosponi Valerio e Lisci Nicolò" (team of 2). App mockups dated "Giovedì,
13 Giugno 2024". `OfficialMedia/` exists.

## Target
- People who can't wake up rested and pleasantly: mostly those with demanding hours (busy professionals, students).

## System
- Alarm unit with no buttons, no sensors and no screen: integrated speaker + microphone; it is also the wireless
  charging base for the bracelet.
- Wearable: the bundled bracelet, or a smartwatch the user already owns.
- Connected room devices: smart bulb on the existing ceiling light, aroma diffuser in a socket by the bed, smart
  roller shutters.
- Connections: app ↔ alarm Bluetooth/Wi-Fi; alarm ↔ wearable and room devices Bluetooth; night noise detection (mic).
- Every interaction happens in the phone app. The alarm switches itself off when the wearable senses you got up.

## Wearable roles
- Wake you in a light sleep phase (non-traumatic wake-up).
- Know when you are awake: the time shows on the alarm only when awake; alarm off once you're up.
- Collect sleep data: phases, duration, awakenings, snoring, body movement, heart rate, body temperature, blood oxygen.

## Experience
- Before sleep: a phone notification that it's time for bed; then soft lights, natural aromas, relaxing sounds.
- Wake-up: gradual light, sound, aroma, daylight through the shutters; afterwards a short sleep questionnaire in the app.
- Feedback on the alarm unit (LED): routine active, no routine active, alarm ringing, changes applied from the app,
  time shown (transparent LED, off while asleep).

## App
- Onboarding: welcome, connect the alarm (search / found / connect manually), connect a smartwatch or use the bundled
  bracelet, sign up.
- Routines ("Le tue routine"): e.g. Work 10 PM–6 AM 8 h on weekdays, Weekend 10 PM–7 AM 9 h; toggle per routine.
- Set an alarm in 3 steps: time and days (circular dial bedtime/wake-up showing sleep hours + "Your sleep time is
  enough!"), gradual preferences for falling asleep, for waking up: light colour/intensity, sound (mountain, sea,
  pasture, crickets, birds, river, rain, woods, wind, rooster), aroma (vanilla, honey, wood, lemon, chocolate), shutters.
- Sleep quality: day/week/month, last night's report, sleep phases chart (awake, REM, light, deep); suggestions to
  adjust the routine from statistics + periodic questionnaires.

## Sustainability
- No screen; transparent LED time, off while sleeping. Recycled aluminium body, zero-impact wood shell. Gradual light
  saves energy compared with switching on at full power.

## Market
- Similar product: Philips SmartSleep (wake-up light).
